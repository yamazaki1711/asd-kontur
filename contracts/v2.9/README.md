# Contract Pack v2.9

Additive consultant NTD corpus contract for `NTD-CONSULTANT-CORPUS-ACCESS-01`.
It preserves Contract Pack v2.8 and the separation between the read-only
Construction Consultant and the schema-bound Domain Harness.

The consultant sees two distinct normative layers: verified provisions and
searchable source text from already admitted artifacts. Searchable source text
does not become a verified provision, active rule, or automatic decision. The
contract distinguishes document presence, bytes, page inventory, text/search
coverage, structured provisions and edition-currency verification.

`ConstructionConsultantQualityReady` remains false until the owner repeats the
personal dialogue acceptance on the deployed merge commit.
