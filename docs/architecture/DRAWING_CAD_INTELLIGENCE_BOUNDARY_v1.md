# Drawing/CAD Intelligence Boundary v1

Drawing Intelligence is currently `CONTRACT_ONLY`. No implemented viewer or
synthetic geometry fixture proves numeric or CAD capability.

The future chain is native/vector metadata and deterministic geometry first,
then raster tiles/segmentation, then bounded BF16 Qwen candidates only where
needed. Every coordinate carries source version, sheet/layout, page/viewport,
CRS, units, precision, transform and locator. Verified design and as-built
geometry require deterministic checks and qualified confirmation.

The viewer may display PDF.js pages, raster tiles and CAD-derived vector
overlays, but display pixels are not canonical geometry. `ezdxf` is a qualified
candidate for DXF adapters; its known 3D and rendering limits require a
representative corpus. DWG conversion, AutoLISP/Python-CAD adapters and external
engines require separate license/security/fidelity decisions.

Executive schemes remain `GeneratedDocumentCandidate` until geometry,
template, layout/render, professional review and explicit finalization pass.
This boundary preserves a future CAD/AI integration contract without claiming
current capability.
